(Memory: project_stale_installed_jit_binary.)
- **Do NOT read `$?` after a pipeline ending in `tail`/`grep`/`head` — it is the pipe's last command, not the checker.** This faked "exit: 0" for failing gate runs in the deferred battery (caught by the user), and two workers independently hit the same trap.
